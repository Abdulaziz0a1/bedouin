# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences. Primary: Saudi travelers and families seeking authentic rural escapes — farm stays, desert camps, heritage houses — who want something real, not a hotel chain. They are browsing from Riyadh or Jeddah, often planning weekend getaways. Secondary: Saudi landowners and rural community hosts who want to earn income from their property and share their heritage.

## Product Purpose

Bedouin is Saudi Arabia's marketplace for authentic rural hospitality. It connects travelers with locally-owned farms, Bedouin camps, heritage houses, and desert retreats across the Kingdom. Success looks like: a traveler booking an experience that feels genuinely Saudi, not touristic; a host whose property becomes an income stream because of Bedouin's trust and approval system.

## Brand Personality

Warm authority. Quiet luxury. Saudi pride.

Three words: rooted, curated, generous.

Emotionally: the feeling of a trusted local recommendation from someone who knows Saudi Arabia deeply. Not a global platform that happens to have Saudi listings — a Saudi platform first.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (Webflow templates, hero-metric grids, startup cream backgrounds)
- Fake listing cards or placeholder content — any mock data that implies scale you don't have
- Airbnb's generic global aesthetic — Bedouin is *specifically* Saudi, not just "travel"
- Booking.com / Expedia density and transactional coldness
- Repeated carousel rows that feel like a category page, not a homepage
- Over-reliance on user-uploaded images to carry the visual weight

## Design Principles

1. **Saudi heritage is the design.** The visual language should feel like it emerged from the landscape, not from a UI kit. Warm stone, gold light, deep shadow.
2. **Editorial over inventory.** When listings are sparse, the page should feel curated, not empty. Story and destination content carries equal weight to real listings.
3. **Trust through restraint.** Show less, promise more. A homepage that carefully presents one featured experience builds more confidence than eight carousels.
4. **Real over hypothetical.** No fake data, no illustrative placeholders passed off as listings. If a section has no real data, hide it or replace it with value messaging.
5. **Bilingual by design.** RTL/LTR parity is not an afterthought. Arabic should feel as premium as English — same hierarchy, same weight.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Arabic RTL must mirror layout correctly. Reduced motion via `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color contrast on dark cinematic backgrounds must meet 4.5:1 for body text.
